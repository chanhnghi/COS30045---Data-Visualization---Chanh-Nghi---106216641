THE SIZE EFFECT

Two fewer viewing hours each day

Median projected saving across all 3,990 complete model rows.

WHY ANY TWO HOURS?

In this model, every two-hour reduction between 2 and 10 hours gives the same saving for the same TV. The formula holds active standby time fixed and replaces viewing time with passive standby time.
